# Material 3 Expressive theme foundation on material3 1.5.0-alpha18

Phase F1 moves the Expressive theme onto `MaterialExpressiveTheme` with the expressive motion scheme, the full M3E shape scale, and the token type scale with real emphasized styles. It pins material3 to 1.5.0-alpha18 and generates tonal-spot schemes and harmonized answer colors from six palette seeds. This pass re-checks the eight issues of the previous NEEDS_CHANGES review, and every fix holds against the code, the alpha18 AAR, the 14 re-recorded screenshots, and a test run on the final tree. The main new finding comes from the Again fix itself: Again now turns at most 5°, but Hard still turns up to 15°, so with blue-side primaries the two warm ratings end up closer than before.

**Watch for:** **confirmed** in the blue (default), violet, and rose themes Hard now sits 28.6° from Again instead of 36–39°, dark Hard is a rust brown (#753403) beside Again's crimson (#930019), and `TonalColorSchemeTest` checks only Again against Good; **confirmed** none of the components that received the caption and chip fixes is rendered in a screenshot or read by a test. Both are non-blocking.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

The Home greeting, the Transfer heading, and both Account headings set SemiBold explicitly, as every tab title does; the Account edits change weight and corners only, not sign-in or password handling. What still takes its weight from the theme is titleLarge, in the Account and Maintenance top app bars, three dialog titles, and two status lines, which go from Medium 20 sp to the token's Regular 22 sp and fall under the deferred per-screen title work. `ExpressiveTypography` binds the Expressive constructor through a single explicit style that is exactly alpha18's display-large-emphasized token, so all 30 styles are the tokens, and the contract test pins them. alpha18's baseline displayLarge tracking is −0.2 sp; the previous pass quoted −0.25 sp.

Light answer containers are capped at chroma 24, which brings the blue theme's Good container (#C2ECD3) level with the other three, and Again turns at most 5°, so it stays red. Hard still turns up to 15°, and for primaries from 244° through 0° to 64°, blue, violet, and rose among them, it turns toward Again: the pair that stood 36–39° apart now sits 28.6° apart in those three themes (**confirmed**). Giving Hard the same 5° limit would keep every theme at 38.6°.

The swatch now previews the generated primary, the `_dynamic` screenshots are gone, and component defaults stay at the M3 spec by decision (4 dp text fields, menus, and snackbars, 8 dp chips, next to 28–32 dp cards), as the alpha18 tokens and the new Account and component screenshots show. The 177-test run, the screenshots, and ktlint all ran after the last edit at 18:28:20. Those tests pin the role pairs, but not the components that draw them (**confirmed**).

### Hard's 15° turn toward Again after the 5° cap

With MCU's CAM16 viewing conditions (checked against MCU's reference hues for pure red, green, and blue), the rating sources sit at Again 25.1°, Hard 63.7°, Good 149.1°, and Easy 254.0°. `harmonizedAnswerPalettes` turns each toward the primary by half the difference, up to these limits:

```kotlin
AgainRating to AGAIN_MAX_ROTATION,    // 5°
HardRating to MAX_HARMONIZE_ROTATION, // 15°
GoodRating to MAX_HARMONIZE_ROTATION,
EasyRating to MAX_HARMONIZE_ROTATION,
```

With a primary on the green side (green 150.6°, teal 185.7°, amber 84.5°), both warm ratings turn upward, and the fix widens their gap from 38.6° to 48.6° (from 34.0° to 44.0° for amber). With the blue seed at 248.3°, violet at 299.0°, or rose at 359.8°, both turn toward red: Again to 20.1°, Hard to 48.7°. The gap there was 38.6° (36.2° for rose) while Again also turned up to 15°, and it is now 28.6° (**confirmed**). `dark_colors.png` shows it for blue: Again #930019 beside Hard #753403, a rust brown where the old fixed Hard was the olive brown #6A3E00. In light mode the pair is #FFDAD7 against #FFDCC8. For a dynamic primary near 34° the gap bottoms out at 19.3°, and the cap does not change that case, because Again's half-difference turn there is already under 5°.

The buttons carry their labels, so this weakens the color coding without failing WCAG 1.4.1. The balance test asserts only that Again and Good stay more than 60° apart and never constrains the pair that harmonization pulls together. Capping Hard at 5° as well keeps all six themes at 38.6° and any primary at 28.6° or more, and it lifts the worst Hard–Good gap from 55.4° to 65.4°, at the price of Hard following the primary less. Either way, a minimum Again–Hard separation in `TonalColorSchemeTest` would pin the decision.

### Typography binding in the alpha18 bytecode

In the compiled `ExpressiveThemeKt`, `Typography()` calls the 15-parameter constructor with every default (mask 32767), and that constructor hands its baseline styles on as the emphasized ones, as the KDoc says. `Typography(displayLargeEmphasized = …)` calls the 30-parameter constructor with mask 0x3FFF7FFF, in which only bit 15, display large emphasized, is explicit. `TypographyTokens` builds DisplayLarge and DisplayLargeEmphasized as `DefaultTextStyle` copies that set the same five fields, and `TypeScaleTokens` gives the emphasized one the Brand family, Medium, 57/64 sp, and zero tracking, so the baseline copy at Medium and 0 sp is the token itself. All 15 emphasized rows in `ExpressiveThemeContractTest` match `TypeScaleTokens` field by field.

Across the Expressive screens and the migration screen, every display and headline text other than the celebration emoji sets its weight. The titleLarge texts without one are the two top app bar titles (the Maintenance one is that screen's heading), the "Support AnkiDroid", "Help", and gesture-picker dialog titles, and the two `StatusText` live regions.
